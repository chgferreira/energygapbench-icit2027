# Directed related-work audit

**Historical directed audit, cutoff: 31 October 2026.** This was a directed check of supplied papers and official implementations, not a systematic review proving an unoccupied research gap. Source metadata and commits were read during implementation. Scientific novelty remains unvalidated.

[TSI-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12747) already provides unified imputation experimentation and downstream evaluation; the inspected arXiv record is a preprint, with no ICLR acceptance asserted. [Bridging Smart Meter Gaps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07276) directly precedes smart-meter imputation comparisons including foundation models and artificial gaps; no first-benchmark claim is available here. [Copy-Paste Imputation](https://github.com/KIT-IAI/CopyPasteImputation) is prior energy-aware cumulative reconstruction, with LGPL terms and a daily donor/Prophet workflow different from the explicit adapted interval track here.

[R24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05980) has a6 February2026 revision recording TMLR and evaluates time-indexed TabPFN-TS/MoTM. Its [published source](https://github.com/taharnbl/tsfm_imputation) was inspected: Fourier features and context-only targets are central, MoTM expects INR weights/config bundles, and a generic tabular regressor is not a faithful replacement. Its dataset results do not imply leadership on these sites.

[TS-ICL](https://github.com/EDF-Lab/ts-icl) provides native imputation and marginal quantiles; the inspected metadata still identify a preprint, and code/weights terms are noncommercial. [CBiT](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/fan26h.html) is confirmed at ICML2026 and uses compressed history. A usable author-linked implementation was not verified in this audit; no stub is described as a reproduction.

BRITS/SAITS/CSDI are established references; ImputeFormer/T1 emphasize dependencies across channels, motivating explicit D=1 audits. [PyPOTS](https://docs.pypots.com/en/latest/pypots.imputation.html) is an implementation source rather than evidence that a wrapper meets this protocol. [MOMENT](https://github.com/moment-timeseries-foundation-model/moment) masked reconstruction and [OpenFIM](https://github.com/FIM4Science/OpenFIM) temporal versus pointwise variants require separate mask/checkpoint audits.

Decisions introduced here are availability-cut queries, realistic gaps fitted only on training, a reusable uncertain bidirectional correction wrapper, fixed downstream emission times and reproducible crash recovery. These are proposed design choices; this audit does not establish that each is absent from every preceding paper. The preserved [R1–R28 inventory](references.md) supplies source pointers; publication/license details require verification before manuscript release. Focused narrows the evidence to point recovery and conditional correction; full expands the method/missingness comparison. Neither design establishes novelty from its size alone. Further review is tracked in [development status](development_status.md).
