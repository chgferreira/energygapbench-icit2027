# Information availability and leakage boundary

Both experiment profiles share this boundary. The focused/full scope and epoch
selection budgets differ, but a larger budget never grants future information.

Task R reconstructs a finished gap at reconnection issuance. Context ends at the
first admitted received right boundary; no later measurement/arrival is visible.
C ends at the latest target instant, without a right boundary or later counter.
O is an explicit offline extension with positive look-ahead. C/O comparisons are
not completed current-profile rankings. Forecast truth is original support at
physical issuance+horizon with frozen matching tolerance and deterministic ties;
missing targets are never interpolated.

P supplies power/masks/timestamps/calendar. A would add real same-installation
auxiliaries; E adds only admissible cumulative import/export boundary readings;
AE combines them. Energy interval metadata identifies endpoint source records,
not hidden interior counter increments. Delayed endpoints remove a constraint
until receipt. P and E answer different information questions and require separate
labels; improvement can include the value of additional energy information.

Artificial masks hide the complete fold/condition/seed stream before extracting
contexts. An unavailable point stays hidden across overlapping queries. A communication
blackout removes intermediate channels; retaining auxiliaries would be a separately
identified channel-failure extension. Observation age is recomputed after hiding.
Scalers and seasonal statistics fit train only.

`build_query` returns a frozen `Query` and a separate `EvaluationTruth`. Only sanitized
Query content reaches inference. Truth is NaN outside originally observed,
artificially hidden target-episode positions. Natural gaps/padding are not truth.
Available observations must be preserved. No original raw collection, pre-mask
window statistics, or hidden counter features are passed into the method.

The focused epoch panel accepts only duration/mask-seed/gap-ID metadata, rejecting
extra value/score fields. Shared site/fold membership is fixed without model errors
or test outcomes. Panel-based checkpoint selection does not replace complete HPO
ranking or final evaluation. Panel identity and coverage are persisted.

Tests cover future/truth poisoning, delayed counters/late arrivals, blackout auxiliary
removal, global hiding in overlapping windows, train isolation, natural-missing
exclusion, fixed-time splits, and selected native wrappers. These checks establish
the tested pipeline boundary, not automatic correctness of every external method.
Remaining hardware/integration evidence is tracked in [development status](development_status.md).
